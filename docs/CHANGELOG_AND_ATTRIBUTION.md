# Attribution and version separation

**2024 coursework and published research:** Original group work. The historic
`coding.ipynb` was independently authored by Jing Xiang Ong, according to his
firsthand recollection; the `RoughCode1`–`RoughCode4` and `roughCode5` notebooks
were developed collaboratively with Abhinav Jain. Other coauthors joined the
2025 IEEE publication. This public package does not contain those five notebooks.

**2026 standalone implementation:** `reproduction/` includes newly authored
AI-assisted Python modules and new notebooks that explore a *similar research
question* with fresh code and improved methodological boundaries. This work
was not used to produce the 2025 published tables. It has not been shown to
reproduce historic results and does not claim authorship of the original
collaborators' contributions.

**Why the distinction matters:** "reimplemented" does not mean "rewrote the
same files to conceal collaborative authorship." Cite the underlying paper,
retain its authors and independently validate any new results.
